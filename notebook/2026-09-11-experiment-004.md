# 2026-09-11 — experiment 004 runs: the suicide convention is observable at 1×4

**Author:** Joseph J. Piché
**Models:** Claude Fable 5.1 (`claude-fable-5-1`) for the design, the
launches, the checks recorded here and this entry; Claude Opus 5
(`claude-opus-5`) for the implementation of `superko-rules`,
`superko-graph`, the CLI and the Lean oracle, and for the two review passes,
run as one workflow

The Rust mirror of `Defs.lean` was built today from
[`../docs/plans/rules-mirror-plan.md`](../docs/plans/rules-mirror-plan.md)
and experiment 004 started running. This entry records what the first runs
found, a hand argument they refuted, and a check made in scratch that a
ledger row now leans on.

## The finding

From the empty 1×4 board under positional superko, the mirror gives
2 098 407 841 games under the paper's suicide-removal convention, which is
Tromp–Farnebäck's Table 7 figure exactly, and 719 178 893 under
`Defs.lean`'s no-suicide rule. On 1×1 through 1×3 the two conventions agree
and both match the table. Every number is `computed` (results/, experiment
004).

So the hypothesis of experiment 004 was wrong as stated, and its second
falsification row fired. The hand argument in
[`2026-09-11-c9-primary-sources.md`](2026-09-11-c9-primary-sources.md)
claimed a position-changing self-capture on a line needs six points; it
reasoned about the flanking enemy chains and forgot the board edge on the
mover's own side. From `.OX.` Black plays the right end. The new chain of
two touches the white stone and the edge and nothing else, the white stone
keeps its liberty on the left, so nothing is captured, and under suicide
removal the black stones leave and the position is `.O..`, which the game
need not have visited. Under `Defs.lean` the play is refused. Left standing
as a worked example of why this project does not trust hand arguments about
its own definitions.

## Why 2×2 should still agree, and why that is a conjecture

The enumerator's census under suicide removal has eight more non-self-loop
edges on 2×2 than under the no-suicide rule. A scratch enumeration listed
them: each is a fourth stone onto a board holding three of the mover's
color, and each returns the empty board. From the empty root the empty
board is archived from the first move, so under PSK none of the eight can
ever be played, and the two conventions should give the same 2×2 count.
That is C-38, `conjecture`: the census is computed, the inference about the
root is an argument, and the two 2×2 runs are what turn it into `computed`.
Tromp's `2x2.c` rejects both suicide shapes outright and its author's
comment says none arises on 2×2, which agrees with the argument and is not
evidence for it.

## Two things the build changed on the way

`clear` is not order-independent. The design assumed that removing dead
stones in place while scanning gives the same result as `Defs.lean`'s
`clear`, which tests every point against the original board, and proposed a
test to record the agreement. The test failed: on the 2×2 board of four
black stones an in-place sweep removes one stone and hands the other three a
liberty, while `clear` removes all four. The position is unreachable in
play, and the function is still a different function. The mirror reads the
original board, as the Lean does, and the disagreement is kept as a test.

The Lean oracle reaches less of the game tree than planned. Walking the tree
through the computable twins in the Lean interpreter costs roughly ten to
twenty times more per extra ply, and the trace depth had to be cut to two
plies on the four-point boards and zero on the six-point ones; the only
Lean-side game count in the repository is 9, on 1×2. The board tables —
every position, color and point of every board with at most six points —
are complete, and every row agreed with the mirror on first replay. All of
it is graded `observed`.

## Running

The 2×2 runs under PSK, both conventions, seven threads each, were launched
at commit `dbeac39` and are in progress. The 1×4 rate is about
6 × 10⁷ nodes per second per thread, and the 1×4 ratio of nodes to games is
three, so each run is projected at 1.2 × 10¹² nodes. A projection, not a
measurement, and experiment 004's fourth row is the recorded outcome if it
does not finish.
